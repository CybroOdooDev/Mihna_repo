1. Global Settings & Interview Configuration

Navigate to Recruitment → Configuration → Settings to activate the Linda AI Interview suite. Configure primary LLM providers, assign default voice synthesizer models, and establish company-wide interview guardrails with instant links to Providers, Question Bank, and Rubrics.

2. AI & Speech Providers Menu

Access the comprehensive AI Interview (Linda) → AI & speech providers view. Manage specialized AI adapters across roles: Anthropic Claude for deep reasoning and live interview dialogue, Deepgram for speech-to-text, and ElevenLabs for natural conversational audio generation.

3. Provider Setup & Instant Connectivity Test

Configure API credentials, target models (e.g. claude-haiku-4-5), and role assignments. Click the Test Connection button to perform a live handshake ping with the AI server, providing instant visual confirmation with a green notification banner.

4. Role-Tailored Interview Templates & Section Builder

Build modular, multi-phase interview templates (e.g., Fresher Odoo Developer – First Round). Define distinct sequential sections including warm-up questions, interactive coding challenges, learn-and-apply exercises, and conversational voice rounds with customized time allocations.

5. Curated Question Bank & Classification

Maintain a centralized library of technical questions categorized by type: Coding, Learn and apply, Voice, and Written English. Each item includes approval status badges, target job roles, difficulty levels, and hidden verification test cases.

6. AI-Assisted Question Generator Wizard

Expand your technical questions instantly using the Generate Pool Questions wizard. Specify target topics, role profiles, and difficulty levels; the AI generates high-quality technical challenges, reference solutions, and test assertions ready for review.

7. Objective Multi-Dimensional Rubric Criteria

Standardize candidate evaluation across four foundational dimensions: Attitude & Professionalism, Coding Competency, Learning Agility, and Oral English Communication. Ensures consistent, unbiased scoring across all applicants.

8. Candidate Privacy & Consent Notices

Comply with global privacy regulations (GDPR & local data protection laws). Maintain version-controlled consent notices informing candidates about AI evaluation, audio recording, data retention policies, and explicit opt-in agreements.

9. High-Volume Intake Recruitment Campaigns

Manage high-volume campus hiring or batch recruitment drives using Intake Campaigns. Monitor invitations sent, completion rates, and average scores from an active dashboard to streamline candidate throughput.

10. One-Click AI Interview Dispatch from Applicant Pipeline

Seamlessly embedded into standard Odoo Recruitment. On any applicant card (e.g. Sara - Trainee), recruiters can dispatch an invitation with a single click using the dedicated Send AI Interview button.

11. Real-Time Smart Button Counters & Chatter Logs

Every dispatched interview increments the AI Interviews smart counter button and logs a detailed message into the Odoo chatter with direct links to the generated candidate session.

12. Responsive Candidate Invitation Email

Candidates receive an automated, mobile-friendly invitation email containing interview expectations, section overviews, and a secure, time-limited Start the interview access token.

13. Candidate Portal - Welcome & Privacy Agreement

Candidates land on a clean, modern portal welcoming them by name. Linda explains the interview layout, equipment requirements, and presents clear privacy terms requiring candidate acknowledgment before entering.

14. Pre-Flight Audio & Hardware System Verification

An automated pre-flight technical check validates browser compatibility, requests microphone permissions, and tests real-time audio input levels to ensure candidates experience zero disruptions during the voice round.

15. Candidate Interview Submission & Completion

Upon completing the final section, candidate answers, code solutions, and audio recordings are securely synchronized with Odoo. A friendly confirmation message informs the applicant that results are under review.

16. Recruiter "To Review" Triage Dashboard

Recruiters access the dedicated To Review triage queue. Completed interviews display aggregate scores (e.g. 60.00 / 100), recommendation badges (Advance, Borderline, Reject), risk flags, and red flag counters.

17. In-Depth Candidate Scorecard & Dimension Breakdown

Drill down into the session form's Scores tab to inspect granular sub-scores across all evaluation dimensions, risk indicators (paste speed, AI assistance cues), sandbox execution outputs, and scheduled reviewer activities.

18. Human-in-the-Loop Review Gate & Hiring Decision

Human recruiters retain ultimate decision authority. Under the Review tab, choose the final outcome (Advance to technical round, Reject, or Keep on file), document feedback, and click Confirm review.

19. Locked Review Confirmation & Audit Traceability

Once confirmed, the session shifts to Reviewed state. Reviewer identity, timestamps, and decision notes are permanently locked into the audit trail, automatically marking recruiter schedule tasks as done.

20. Automated Pipeline Stage Advancement

Upon confirming advancement, Linda automatically moves the applicant from the initial interview stage to the next recruitment pipeline column (e.g. Second Interview / Technical Round), eliminating manual stage drag-and-drop.

21. Comprehensive Applicant Record with AI Scorecard

The candidate's profile in Odoo Recruitment permanently retains the composite score, recommendation status, and link to the full evaluation scorecard, empowering hiring managers during final decision-making.

What is Linda AI Interview, and how does it work?

Linda is an automated AI interviewer built directly inside Odoo 19. It dispatches first-round technical screening interviews via secure email invitations. Candidates enter a browser portal where Linda leads them through written questions, live Python coding challenges executed in a safe sandbox, and an interactive voice round. Once complete, Linda scores the candidate against objective rubrics and presents the findings to your recruiters for human review.

Which AI and speech providers are supported?

The module features an extensible provider architecture. Out of the box, it supports Anthropic Claude (Claude 3.5 Haiku and Sonnet), OpenAI (GPT-4o), and Groq for core LLM evaluation and dialogue. For audio synthesis and transcription, it connects with Deepgram and ElevenLabs. You can also configure test pings directly from Odoo to verify API credentials at any time.

Does Linda make hiring decisions automatically?

No. Linda acts as an intelligent assistant that does the heavy lifting of conducting and scoring interviews. All final decisions remain strictly with your hiring managers and recruiters through our "Human-in-the-Loop" review gate. Recruiters can view all evidence, transcripts, and code runs before confirming an advance or rejection.

How does anti-cheat and AI-assistance detection work?

During the interview, the web portal records keystroke velocity, copy-paste bursts, and answer cadence. Answers are also scanned for telltale markers of generative AI. If suspicious activity is detected, Linda flags the session with an anomaly warning and increments a red flag counter for recruiter review.

Can we customize interview templates and evaluation rubrics?

Yes, completely. You have full control over templates, section sequences, time limits, question pools, and rubric criteria weights. You can maintain distinct templates for freshers, senior engineers, project managers, and QA specialists.

Is the module compatible with both Community and Enterprise?

Yes. Linda – AI Interview is built natively on Odoo 19 and functions seamlessly in both Odoo Community and Odoo Enterprise environments.
